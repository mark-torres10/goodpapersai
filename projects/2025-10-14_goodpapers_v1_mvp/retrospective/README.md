## Purpose

Retrospectives capture learnings, successes, and areas for improvement after major milestones or ticket completions.

## Structure

Individual ticket retrospectives will be added here as:
- `PER-8_setup_retrospective.md`
- `PER-9_backend_retrospective.md`
- `PER-10_arxiv_integration_retrospective.md`
- etc.

A final project retrospective will be created after V1 launches:
- `v1_launch_retrospective.md`

---

## Retrospective Template

Each retrospective should include:
1. **What Went Well** - Successes and positive outcomes
2. **What Could Be Improved** - Challenges and areas for growth
3. **Action Items** - Specific improvements for next time
4. **Time Tracking** - Estimated vs actual time
5. **Technical Insights** - Discoveries and learnings
